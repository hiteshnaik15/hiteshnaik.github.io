What I do

Positioning

I take AI and data platforms from a product thesis to something an enterprise runs on: the roadmap, the governance, the adoption curve and the cost model, with engineering and data science in the same room.

Sixteen years across telecom, retail and fintech, most recently as a Staff Product Manager at Walmart Global Tech, where I owned the Enterprise Feature Store and the Unified Data Pipeline used across finance, retail and supply chain. Before that, nine years at Huawei moving telecom OSS platforms onto microservices and cloud, leading a 12-person cross-functional team and delivering on site across Asia-Pacific, the Gulf Cooperation Council, West Africa and Europe.

Today I am founding Talking Trees Agro, a satellite and AI crop-intelligence platform for smallholder farmers. It is the same discipline at a different scale: agentic workflows that watch the data, models with explicit evaluation criteria, and a human in the loop wherever a prediction is not confident enough to act on.
